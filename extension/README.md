# SumItUp - Floating AI Web Summarizer (Chrome Extension)

A Grammarly & Shazam-style floating AI widget that works over any website on your laptop. Click the glowing bubble to automatically summarize the webpage or selected text using Google Gemini.

---

## 🚀 Quick Setup (Takes 15 Seconds)

1. Open **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, **Arc**, or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. In the address bar, navigate to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions
   ```
   *(Or `brave://extensions` / `edge://extensions`)*
3. Enable **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner.
5. Select this `extension` folder:
   ```text
   /Users/mahant/Desktop/SUM-IT-UP/extension
   ```
6. **Done!** 🎉

---

## Features

- **Floating Grammarly / Shazam Icon**: A discreet, glowing circular icon floating in the bottom-right corner of every website.
- **Draggable Anywhere**: Drag and position the icon anywhere on your screen.
- **1-Click Auto Summarization**: Reads and cleans the page content (strips ads, menus, sidebars) and generates a structured summary with Gemini.
- **Selected Text Summarization**: Highlight any paragraph on a webpage and click the icon to summarize just that selection.
- **Multiple Summary Modes**:
  - ⚡ **Quick Overview**: Balanced executive summary.
  - 🎯 **Key Takeaways**: Crisp bullet points of core facts.
  - 🔬 **Deep Dive**: In-depth breakdown with sections.
  - 👶 **Explain Like I'm 5**: Jargon-free simple explanation.
- **Audio Read-Aloud**: Listen to summaries with speech synthesis.
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Cmd + Shift + S` (Mac) or `Ctrl + Shift + S` (Windows/Linux) to trigger instantly.
